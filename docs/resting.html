What to notice

  • With Vm at 0 mV, K⁺ ions leave the cell down their concentration gradient. Each one carries a positive charge out, so the inside becomes negative.
  • As the inside becomes negative it pulls K⁺ back in. Vm stops changing at the Nernst potential, where the electrical pull exactly balances the concentration gradient.
  • At equilibrium, ions still cross in both directions, but at equal rates. The flux plot shows the two rates meeting.
  • Only a tiny number of ions has to cross to charge the membrane, so the concentrations barely change.
  • Try Na⁺ or Cl⁻, swap the inside and outside concentrations, or make them equal.

Interactive neuron simulators

Browser-based models of ion channels and neurons for teaching. Every slider re-runs the simulation instantly, so students build intuition by changing a parameter and watching what happens.

The tools are ordered from single channels up to whole-neuron models. Work through them in turn, or jump straight to the one you need.

1 · Single channels

Stochastic Channel Simulation

Start here: watch individual channels flicker open and shut at random, and see smooth currents emerge as you add more of them.
2 · Channel kinetics

Zoo of Ion Channels

Compare K⁺, Na⁺, persistent Na⁺ and HCN channels side by side, each simulated both stochastically and as an ODE.
3 · The action potential

Hodgkin-Huxley Model

Combine Na⁺ and K⁺ channels into a full neuron. Run current- and voltage-clamp experiments and see how each conductance shapes the spike.
4 · Reduced models

FitzHugh-Nagumo Model

Reduce the neuron to two variables and read excitability straight off the phase plane, from nullclines and trajectories.
5 · Firing patterns

Izhikevich Model

Reproduce 20 canonical cortical firing patterns, from regular spiking to bursting, with four parameters.
